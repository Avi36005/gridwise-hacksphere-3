How validation works

  1. Reserve the last 20% of labeled rows as a final holdout.
  2. Compare models on three past-to-future folds within the first 80%.
  3. Evaluate only the selected model on the untouched holdout.
  4. Refit that model on all 4,000 labeled rows for the app.

The supplied 1,000-row sample has no demand labels. Its forecast accuracy cannot be measured.
