NO GUESSWORK IN THE SWITCHING LOGIC

Three rules.
Every signal explained.

The dashboard applies these rules to each predicted hour. Battery simulation then shows whether storage can meet the requested demand.

01 / DEMAND> 160 kW

Request renewable storage.

Demand above 160 kW triggers a storage signal. At exactly 160 kW, the evening rule still applies.

02 / EVENING WINDOW18:00–22:00

Prioritize storage in the evening.

Every hour from 18:00 through 22:00 requests storage, regardless of predicted demand.

03 / OVERLOAD≥ 200 kW

Flag a critical overload.

An independent alert appears at or above 200 kW. The alert does not replace the dispatch signal.

Otherwise, the signal is main grid. Simulated grid support covers storage shortfalls caused by battery reserve or power limits.
